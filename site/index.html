02 / Sources and integrity

Data: NFL weekly player box-score data from the nflverse-data project, subject to source availability and licensing. The model does not have live Bet365 odds, weather, depth charts or injury updates.

Backtests: Prospective evaluation excludes the selected current week. The retrospective league-wide test avoids using future numeric observations; however, weekly-player records alone cannot reconstruct exact pregame rosters, active/inactive status, or tradable lines.

Reliability: The probability method remains experimental, using smoothed rolling empirical rates, a parametric distribution component, a league reference, and a shrunken Gaussian-copula dependence estimate. Sensitivity bands are not calibrated statistical confidence intervals.

Betting: Edge Finder is independent of Bet365 and the NFL. It places no wagers and cannot certify a profitable strategy.
